**Headline:** Behavioral Support for Group Home Settings

We partner with group homes across Maryland to promote positive behaviors, emotional stability, and a genuinely supportive environment for every resident.

**What this includes:**
- Behavioral Assessments & Care Plans
- Staff Support & Consultation
- Crisis Management Support
- Person-Centered, Strengths-Based Care

### 3d. Sub-Page — DDA Nursing Support Services

**Headline:** DDA Nursing Support Services

Under Maryland's Developmental Disabilities Administration (DDA), we provide nursing support services designed to keep individuals safe, healthy, and well cared for in their home and community settings.

**Services include:**
- **Nursing Consultation** — expert nursing guidance for individuals and support teams
- **Health Case Management** — coordinated oversight of health needs across providers and settings
- **Nursing Delegation** — training and oversight enabling direct support professionals to safely carry out specific nursing tasks
