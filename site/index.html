What we do

Five ways we walk alongside you

Every service is delivered with a person-centered approach — built around each individual's needs, not a one-size-fits-all model.

Mental Health Care

Comprehensive psychiatric evaluations, medication management, and ongoing support for children, adolescents, and adults — including telehealth.

Learn more

Care for Medically Fragile Individuals

Specialized emotional and behavioral support for people with complex medical conditions — in close collaboration with medical teams and families.

Learn more

Group Home Behavioral Support

Partnering with group homes to promote positive behaviors, emotional stability, and genuinely supportive environments for every resident.

Learn more

DDA Nursing Support Services

Nursing consultation, health case management, nursing delegation, HRST screening, and clinical review for individuals under Maryland's Developmental Disabilities Administration.

Learn more

Meet our founder

Led by experience, grounded in compassion

Chance For Life Inc. was founded by Oluwakemi Fagbuyi, MSN, PMHNP-BC, a board-certified Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner with extensive experience caring for children, adolescents, adults, and older adults.

She understands the connection between physical health, emotional well-being, and quality of life — and she's committed to a safe, supportive, judgment-free space where every person feels heard and valued.
