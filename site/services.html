DDA Nursing Support

Keeping individuals safe, healthy & well cared for

Under Maryland's Developmental Disabilities Administration (DDA), we provide nursing support services designed to keep individuals safe and healthy in their home and community settings.

  • Nursing ConsultationExpert nursing guidance for individuals and support teams.
  • Health Case ManagementCoordinated oversight of health needs across providers and settings.
  • Nursing DelegationTraining and oversight enabling direct support professionals to safely carry out specific nursing tasks.
  • Health Risk Screening Tool (HRST)Completing and updating health risk screenings to identify changes in health status early.
  • Clinical ReviewerServing as the clinical reviewer on your team — reviewing screenings and providing clinical recommendations.
