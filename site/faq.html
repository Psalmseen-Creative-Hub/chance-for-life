Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our care, training, and how to get started. Don't see your question? We're always happy to talk it through.

We provide mental health care (psychiatric evaluations and medication management), specialized support for medically fragile individuals, behavioral support for group homes, DDA nursing support services (consultation, health case management, delegation, HRST screening, and clinical review), and CMT Training for caregivers.
Yes — telehealth is available in addition to in-person care, serving individuals and families across Maryland.

We are a faith-inspired practice that believes every individual deserves to be treated with dignity, compassion, and hope. Our values are rooted in integrity, kindness, and respect for all people. While our care is guided by these principles, we provide evidence-based mental health services to individuals of all faiths, backgrounds, and beliefs.
